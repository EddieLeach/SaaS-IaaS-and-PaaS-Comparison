# ☁️ Cloud Service Models – SaaS, PaaS, and IaaS Case Study

**🚀 Summary:**  
Analyzed and compared major **cloud service models**—**Software as a Service (SaaS)**, **Platform as a Service (PaaS)**, and **Infrastructure as a Service (IaaS)**—to demonstrate understanding of **vendor responsibility**, **control levels**, and **use-case alignment**.  
Explored **SaaS advantages**, **real-world examples**, and **decision criteria** for selecting the right model in a cloud architecture.

### **🧩 What**
- Defined **SaaS**, including its **characteristics**, **benefits**, and **real-world applications**.  
- Compared **SaaS, PaaS, and IaaS** through a structured diagram outlining control and responsibility layers.  
- Identified **key SaaS examples** such as **Google Workspace**, **Microsoft 365**, **Salesforce**, **Slack**, and **Zoom**.  
- Categorized SaaS types: **General-Purpose**, **Vertical (Industry-Specific)**, **Customizable**, and **Bespoke**.  
- Explained **advantages and challenges** of SaaS from a cloud engineer’s perspective.  

---

## **⚙️ How**

**Tech Stack & Focus Areas:**  
**Cloud Models:** SaaS • PaaS • IaaS  
**Concepts:** Vendor Responsibility • Scalability • Multi-Tenancy • Subscription Models • Security & Maintenance  

- Developed a **comparative table** showing customer vs. provider responsibility for each model.  
- Illustrated **control layers** (Data → Application → O/S → Infrastructure) across deployment types.  
- Outlined decision criteria for when to use **IaaS (maximum control)**, **PaaS (development flexibility)**, or **SaaS (ease of use and maintenance)**.  
- Analyzed **real industry examples** for each model and their ideal use cases.  

---

## **🎯 Why**
To demonstrate understanding of **cloud architecture models** and how each impacts **engineering control**, **deployment strategy**, and **security responsibility**.  
This case study reflects the ability to evaluate **cloud solutions** and select the right service model for **cost, scalability, and business efficiency**.
